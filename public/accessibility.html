Accessibility

Last updated: May 2026

The Tea Cozy is committed to making our website accessible to everyone. We believe every visitor — regardless of ability or the technology they use — deserves a welcoming, inclusive experience.

1. Our Commitment

Actively improving

We are working to ensure that our website conforms to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review our site and make improvements whenever we identify gaps.

2. Measures We've Taken

Steps we have taken to improve accessibility on this site include:

  • Semantic HTML5 landmarks (header, main, nav, footer) to aid screen-reader navigation.
  • Descriptive alt text on all meaningful images.
  • Sufficient colour contrast between text and backgrounds across the site.
  • Keyboard-navigable interactive elements (menus, buttons, links).
  • Responsive design that works across screen sizes and zoom levels.
  • aria-label attributes on navigation landmarks and icon-only buttons.
  • Skip-to-content link available for keyboard users (activate by pressing Tab on page load).

3. Known Limitations

While we strive for full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, there are areas we are still working to improve:

  • Some older images in our gallery may have incomplete or generic alt text — we are adding descriptive alternatives progressively.
  • Third-party embedded content (such as maps or social media widgets) may not fully meet our standards; we are evaluating accessible alternatives.

We are actively addressing these limitations. If you encounter a specific barrier, please let us know using the contact details below.

4. Assistive Technology

Our website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack)
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Browser zoom up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
  • High-contrast and forced-colour modes

5. Feedback & Assistance

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you experience any difficulty accessing content or interacting with any feature, we want to know.

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days. If we cannot resolve an accessibility barrier immediately, we will provide an accessible alternative where possible.

6. Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact a relevant enforcement or oversight body in your jurisdiction. For users in the United States, you may contact the U.S. Department of Justice, ADA National Network at 1-800-514-0301 (voice) or 1-800-514-0383 (TTY).
